Questions

Good to know

Is Fursday available in my city?

Right now, Fursday is live only in Pune and Gurgaon. We're deliberately starting small so every sitter can be properly verified before we expand to more cities.

How are sitters verified?

Every sitter completes Aadhaar eKYC verification before accepting their first booking. We also review their service area, experience, and require a complete profile before approval.

What happens if my sitter cancels last minute?

You're refunded in full immediately, and we'll help you find a replacement sitter as quickly as possible.

How does payment work?

You pay securely through UPI, card, or net banking once your sitter accepts the booking. Your payment is only released to the sitter after the service is completed.

What's included in the Rs.25,000 vet cover?

Every booking on Fursday automatically includes vet insurance cover up to Rs.25,000, in case of an accident or emergency during the service.

How do I become a sitter?

Tap "Become a sitter," tell us what services you'd like to offer and your availability, set your prices, and complete ID verification. Most sitters are live within 24 hours of applying.
